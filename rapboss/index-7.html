HOW TO PLAY

The note is the button.

There are no bottom hit boxes. Tap the falling note itself as it reaches the gold line. The note glows when it enters the scoring window. For a HOLD bar, touch the head and keep your finger down until the tail finishes.

Difficulty

Higher difficulties use denser charts, smaller timing windows and heavier miss damage. Blood Moon is the highest score ceiling.

Note Speed

Speed changes how fast notes travel on screen. It never speeds up the music. Faster speeds earn a larger score multiplier.

Crowd Meter

Misses drain the room. Below 20% is DANGER. Hit zero and the round ends early — unless your one-time LAST BITE saves the set.

Blood Rush

Strong hits charge the Blood Rush bar. Fill it and the room explodes for a temporary score boost and stronger crowd recovery.

Timing offset: 70 ms

Positive values make the notes arrive later. Use this only if Bluetooth/speaker delay makes the visual hit line feel early or late.
